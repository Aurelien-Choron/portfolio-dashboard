Volatility is measured{% if analytics %}, over {{ analytics.window.observations }} weekly returns in euros against {{ analytics.benchmark.label }}{% endif %}. They are not the same kind of number, and the chart is only as good as the first one.
  • Those returns are before the funds' own ongoing charges — the Investments page prices what those cost you each year.
  • The ideal line is the tangency portfolio blended with guaranteed savings until it runs exactly the volatility your portfolio already runs, so the gap between the two is what the construction earns and not what extra risk earns. It is the best mix as measured over one window, found by looking at that window: a benchmark, not a promise.
  • Contributions are assumed invested at the same mix, every month, whatever the market just did. Envelope ceilings are not modelled here — the PEA contribution cap in particular; the Strategy page is the one that tracks it.
  • A band is not a floor. A tenth of the simulations ended below it, and nothing rules out a decade that resembles none of them.
